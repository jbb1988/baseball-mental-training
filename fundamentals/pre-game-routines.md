# Pre-Game Mental Routines

## Overview
A consistent pre-game mental routine helps players enter the optimal performance state before taking the field. This routine should be practiced and refined until it becomes automatic.

## The 15-Minute Pre-Game Mental Routine

### Minutes 1-3: Physical Preparation
- Deep breathing exercises (4-4-4 pattern: inhale 4, hold 4, exhale 4)
- Light stretching while maintaining focus
- Progressive muscle relaxation

### Minutes 4-8: Mental Imagery
- Visualize 3 successful at-bats or defensive plays
- See yourself executing with confidence
- Include all senses (feel the bat, hear the crowd, see the ball)

### Minutes 9-12: Positive Self-Talk
- Repeat personal affirmations ("I am prepared", "I trust my training")
- Review your approach for different situations
- Set 1-2 specific goals for the game

### Minutes 13-15: Focus Transition
- Practice your between-pitch routine
- Get mentally locked into the first pitch
- Final confidence-building statement

## Customization Tips
- Adapt timing based on your position (pitchers may need longer)
- Include position-specific visualizations
- Practice during batting practice to make it automatic
