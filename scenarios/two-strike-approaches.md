# Two-Strike Mental Approaches

## The Mental Challenge
Two-strike situations create the highest pressure moments in hitting. Mental preparation is often more important than physical adjustments.

## Core Mental Principles

### 1. Embrace the Challenge
- "This is where champions are made"
- View it as an opportunity, not a threat
- Remember: pitchers feel pressure too

### 2. Simplify Your Approach
- Focus on making contact, not driving the ball
- Think "see it, hit it" instead of complex mechanics
- Trust your instincts and training

### 3. Control Your Breathing
- One deep breath before stepping in
- Exhale tension, inhale confidence
- Stay loose and athletic

## Visualization Script
*Close your eyes and see yourself in a two-strike count...*

1. You step out, take a breath, and smile slightly
2. You see the pitcher working harder, feeling the pressure
3. You step back in with quiet confidence
4. You see a strike and put a good swing on it
5. You feel the solid contact and see the ball find a gap

## Mental Cues
- **"Battle time"** - embrace the competition
- **"Trust and attack"** - commit to your swing
- **"Controlled aggression"** - stay balanced but competitive

## Recovery from Strikeouts
- Immediate response: "Next at-bat"
- Learn something specific from each at-bat
- Use it as motivation fuel, not negative energy
